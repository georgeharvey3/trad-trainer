# Trad Trainer

A practice tool for traditional Irish tunes. It schedules tunes for review with spaced repetition and raises their tempo over time.

## Language

**Library**:
The shared list of known tunes that the app ingests. It is the same for every user.
_Avoid_: Catalogue, tune bank

**Repertoire**:
The tunes of one user, each with its own review schedule and tempo.
_Avoid_: Tune list, my tunes

**Learning**:
Working through a new tune by playing it over and over until a take can be recorded. It is a separate activity from review.
_Avoid_: Practising (for a new tune)

**Tune type**:
The dance form of a tune, such as reel, jig or slide. The app uses the same twelve types as The Session.
_Avoid_: Genre, rhythm

**Setting**:
One transcription of a tune on The Session. A tune can have many settings, each in its own key.
_Avoid_: Version, arrangement

**Key**:
The key of a Library tune: the key of its first setting on The Session.
