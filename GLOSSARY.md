# Trad Trainer

A practice tool for traditional Irish tunes. It schedules tunes for review with spaced repetition and raises their tempo over time.

## Language

**Library**:
The shared list of known tunes that the app ingests. It is the same for every user.
_Avoid_: Catalogue, tune bank

**Repertoire**:
The tunes of one user, each with its own review schedule and tempo.
_Avoid_: Tune list, my tunes

**Learning**:
Working through a new tune by playing it over and over until a take can be recorded. It is a separate activity from review.
_Avoid_: Practising (for a new tune)

**Stage**:
Where a tune of the Repertoire is in its life: **Learning** or **Rotation**. A tune you can already play goes straight into the Rotation.
_Avoid_: Status, state

**Rotation**:
The tunes of the Repertoire that are in the daily review. Only they join the practice queue.
_Avoid_: Active tunes, review list

**Learnt bars**:
The bars of a Learning tune's score that the user has marked as learnt. When all are learnt, the app asks for a take.
_Avoid_: Parts, sections (the Library has no part data)

**Take**:
A recording of a tune by the user. Recording a full take moves a Learning tune into the Rotation.
_Avoid_: Clip

**Review**:
One grade of one tune: Again, Hard, Good or Easy, with the tempo before and after. The log of reviews gives the streak and the tempo over time.
_Avoid_: Practice event

**Tune type**:
The dance form of a tune, such as reel, jig or slide. The app uses the same twelve types as The Session.
_Avoid_: Genre, rhythm

**Setting**:
One transcription of a tune on The Session. A tune can have many settings, each in its own key.
_Avoid_: Version, arrangement

**Key**:
The key of a Library tune: the key of its first setting on The Session.
